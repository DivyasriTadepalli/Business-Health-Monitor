# Business Health Monitor – BrandX India

## Project Overview
Business Health Monitor is a data analytics project focused on evaluating business performance using sales, profitability, inventory, customer satisfaction, and returns data.

The project analyses BrandX India's store inventory and business performance data from 2022 to 2024 to identify trends, understand business challenges, and provide actionable recommendations.

## Project Objectives
- Analyse revenue and sales performance across product categories.
- Evaluate profit margins across categories and sales channels.
- Understand inventory turnover and stock availability.
- Analyse customer satisfaction and product returns.
- Evaluate promotion performance.
- Develop an interactive business performance dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Initial data inspection and quality checks.
- **MySQL:** Data analysis, SQL queries, and business insights.
- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib):** Data analysis and visualisation.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.

## Dataset Overview
- **Dataset:** BrandX India Store Inventory Dataset
- **Period:** 2022–2024
- **Records:** 5,121
- **Columns:** 47

The dataset includes information about stores, products, sales, revenue, profit margins, inventory, customers, promotions, suppliers, and returns.

*Note: The original dataset is not included in this repository.*

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Inspection:** Examined the dataset in Excel to understand its structure and identify data quality issues.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Used Power Query to inspect and prepare the dataset, including reviewing missing values and setting appropriate data types.
3. **SQL Analysis:** Used MySQL to investigate revenue, profit margins, sales channels, customer satisfaction, returns, and inventory performance.
4. **Python Analysis:** Used Pandas and Matplotlib to examine promotion performance and visualise average profit margins and discounts.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Created a three-page interactive Power BI report.

## Key Business Insights

- Electronics and Gadgets together contribute approximately **88.78% of total revenue**.
- Electronics has the highest average customer satisfaction score of **4.41**.
- Groceries has the highest average inventory turnover at **5.41**.
- Profit margins are relatively consistent across product categories and sales channels.
- BOGO promotions show the highest average profit margin among the promotion types analysed.
- Flash Sales have the highest average discount offered.

## Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard contains three pages:

1. **Business Overview:** Overall business performance and key metrics.
2. **Financial & Inventory Analysis:** Revenue, profitability, inventory turnover, and stock availability.
3. **Customer & Returns Analysis:** Customer satisfaction, return rates, and return reasons.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor revenue concentration in Electronics and Gadgets.
- Review inventory planning across categories with lower inventory turnover.
- Investigate customer satisfaction differences across product categories.
- Monitor product return patterns and identify opportunities to improve product quality and customer experience.
- Evaluate promotional strategies by considering both discounts and profitability.
